Turtle Species Info

Five species of sea turtles occur in Florida waters:

  • Loggerhead
  • Green
  • Leatherback
  • Hawksbill
  • Kemp's ridley

Take Action

You can help protect nesting sea turtles and their habitats.

  • Participate in beach cleanups.
  • Reduce plastic waste.
  • Keep nesting beaches dark at night.
  • Respect marked turtle nests.
