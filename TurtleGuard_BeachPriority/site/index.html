How the score works

Priority = 100 × (historical occurrence weight × observed active-year fraction + nesting activity weight × normalized forecast density proxy + urgency weight × season proximity). The nesting forecast is computed from locally run TimesFM 3 where the validation-selected method is TimesFM, otherwise the appropriate historical baseline. Projected totals use only species with compatible forecast years. Count normalization currently compares regions of different survey extents; until reliable survey lengths are joined, the index is illustrative, not comparable ecological density. Seasonal urgency is shared across Florida regions and therefore does not establish which beach will nest first.
Sources: FWC coastline segments, Boca Raton, Volusia, Canaveral NPS. This demo accesses FWC geometry directly from your browser; internet connection is required.
