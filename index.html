TurtleGuard

What is TurtleGuard?

TurtleGuard combines historical sea turtle nesting records, interactive coastline maps and AI forecasting to help people understand Florida's nesting beaches and make informed conservation decisions. Conservation organizations can use these forecasts alongside field surveys to prioritize nesting patrols, turtle-friendly lighting outreach and habitat protection.

Field guide / Species

Meet Florida's sea turtles.

Five species occur in Florida's waters. Loggerhead, green and leatherback turtles nest regularly, while hawksbill and Kemp's ridley nesting is uncommon.

Explore their habitats, nesting seasons, hatching patterns and conservation status. Nesting dates and clutch estimates are approximate.

Learn more from the Florida Fish and Wildlife Conservation Commission and NOAA Fisheries.

Field guide / Conservation

Small actions. Lasting impact.

Everyone can help protect Florida's sea turtles, whether living along the coast or visiting its beaches.

Found an injured or stranded sea turtle?

Contact the Florida Fish and Wildlife Conservation Commission's 24-hour Wildlife Alert Hotline.

1-888-404-FWCC (3922)

Provide the animal's exact location. Do not handle the turtle or return it to the water unless directed by authorized responders.

  • Never touch or move sea turtles, nests or hatchlings without authorization.

  • Join rescue and handling activities only through permitted organizations.

How TurtleGuard works

Historical sea turtle nesting records inform TimesFM forecasts for the 2027 season. Predicted nests per mile allow comparisons across eight directly monitored coastal areas, while additional county estimates are apportioned across mapped beach sections.
